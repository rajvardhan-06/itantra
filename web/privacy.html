Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026 • Version 1.0.0

iTantra was purposefully engineered from the ground up on the principle of Zero-Cloud Data Sovereignty. Our mission is to facilitate reliable, multilingual voice and text communication in low-bandwidth, disconnected, and disaster-impacted environments without relying on third-party servers, remote cloud infrastructure, or tracking systems.

1. Core Principles: No Cloud, No Telemetry

The iTantra Android application does not connect to any central cloud API, remote backend server, or analytics pipeline.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, provide an email, phone number, or identity.
  • Zero Analytics: We include no advertising trackers, crash analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics or Mixpanel), or third-party telemetry libraries.
  • Local Speech Processing: When you speak into Push-to-Talk (PTT), raw voice samples are processed purely in device RAM by local ONNX/TFLite or Vosk speech models. Your audio is never uploaded to any cloud server.

2. Android Permissions & Purpose

iTantra requests only the minimum set of permissions necessary to capture local voice input and negotiate direct device-to-device wireless links:

Permission Type Purpose
RECORD_AUDIO RAM Only Captures microphone audio during Push-to-Talk (PTT) for local Speech-to-Text inference. Audio is immediately discarded from memory once transcribed.
NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES Local P2P Allows peer discovery and direct high-speed socket negotiation over Wi-Fi Direct (Android 13+) without location access.
BLUETOOTH_SCAN & BLUETOOTH_CONNECT Local P2P Discovers nearby iTantra nodes and establishes low-energy Bluetooth mesh communication links (Android 12+).
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION OS Requirement Required by the Android operating system on Android 11 and lower to perform Bluetooth and Wi-Fi hardware scans. iTantra never records, stores, or transmits your GPS coordinates.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS System Displays a foreground service notification to ensure peer-to-peer transport sockets remain active when the screen is locked.

3. Data Storage & Message Retention

Transmitted and received text messages are stored strictly inside your phone's isolated application sandbox in an encrypted local Room SQLite database.

  • Your message history is accessible only to you through the iTantra application UI.
  • You can purge your entire local communication history at any time with a single tap in Settings → Clear Local History.
  • Uninstalling the iTantra application permanently purges all local databases, speech cache, and settings from your device.

4. Peer-to-Peer Transmission Security

When communicating with nearby devices via Wi-Fi Direct or Bluetooth mesh:

  • Packets are dispatched directly to the peer IP address or Bluetooth MAC address within physical radio range.
  • Transmission occurs locally over RF (Radio Frequency) and does not route through the public internet, internet service providers (ISPs), or cellular towers.
  • Each packet contains sequence headers, payload checksums, and compressed text to maximize delivery reliability over intermittent channels.

5. Open Source Transparency

The complete source code of the iTantra Android application and its build configurations are open for inspection and audit on GitHub at github.com/rajvardhan-06/itantra. Anyone can independently inspect, verify, and compile the code from source.

6. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions, architectural inquiries, or security audit feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or iTantra, please open an issue or discussion on the official GitHub repository.
