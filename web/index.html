Core Capabilities

Engineered for Disconnected & Low-Resource Environments

When cellular towers fail or internet infrastructure is absent, iTantra establishes direct, secure voice-to-text communication between nearby devices.

Offline Speech-to-Text

On-device neural acoustic models transcribe voice into text instantly. Audio never leaves the phone's local memory sandbox.

Direct Wireless Transport

Connects over Wi-Fi Direct and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). Creates decentralized peer-to-peer links without cellular data.

Offline Text-to-Speech

Receiving devices convert incoming text into natural voice output using offline TTS engines with customizable pitch and speech rate.

99% Bandwidth Compression

Instead of sending 500 KB of raw PCM voice streaming, iTantra transmits compact ~80-byte structured text micro-payloads over the wire.

CRC32 Wire Verification

Every transmission frame is protected with IEEE 802.3 CRC32 checksums. Corrupt or noisy packets are caught and rejected immediately.

SIH Demo & Fault Injection

Built-in Smart India Hackathon judging controls allow single-device evaluation with loopback peers, CRC corruption injection, and live telemetry.

Zero-Cloud Architecture

The Three-Stage Offline Pipeline

How iTantra delivers voice communication across hardware radios with maximum bandwidth efficiency.

1
Step 1 · Speech Capture & VAD

Local Voice Transcription

AudioRecord captures 16kHz 16-bit mono PCM. Voice Activity Detection (VAD) removes silence, and the on-device acoustic model transcribes speech into UTF-8 text.

2
Step 2 · Micro-Framing & Radio

Zero-Loss Text Framing

Text is framed into binary micro-payloads with header bytes, sequence numbers, priority flags, and IEEE 802.3 CRC32 checksums, then transmitted over Wi-Fi Direct or BLE.

3
Step 3 · Verification & Playback

Local Speech Synthesis

The receiver validates the CRC32 checksum, commits the message to local Room database history, and automatically speaks the text in the recipient's language.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions & Answers

Does iTantra require internet, 4G, or Wi-Fi routers?
No. iTantra was designed specifically for zero-infrastructure environments. It runs on-device speech-to-text neural models and communicates peer-to-peer using Wi-Fi Direct or Bluetooth Low Energy without needing cellular connectivity or internet routers.
Why does Android display a warning when installing the APK?
Android displays a standard security prompt for any application downloaded directly from a browser rather than the Google Play Store. iTantra is verified with APK Signature Scheme v2, contains zero trackers, and collects zero external telemetry.
Are my voice recordings uploaded to any cloud servers?
No. Audio captured during Push-to-Talk is processed strictly in local device RAM by on-device acoustic models. Raw audio is never saved to external storage, uploaded, or transmitted off-device.
How does the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Peer Simulator work?
On the Home and PTT screens, iTantra provides dedicated loopback simulation buttons ("Hindi Peer", "Bengali Peer", "English Peer"). This allows evaluators and judges to test the complete decoding, CRC32 verification, and text-to-speech pipeline on a single phone without requiring a secondary physical device.
